What to do in Barcelona on a Monday night

Monday is the quietest night in Barcelona. Most people think nothing's on. Here's the plan when you still want to go out.

Short answer: go to Bad Decisions, the Monday night party at Sala Apolo (C/ Nou de la Rambla 113, Poble-sec). Every Monday from midnight to 5am, with hip-hop, house and pop throwbacks. Free list until 01:30, tickets from 11 €. 18+.

Is there nightlife in Barcelona on Mondays?

Yes, but not much. Most big clubs in Barcelona focus on Thursday to Saturday, so Monday is usually the quietest night of the week. That's exactly why we picked it.

Monday is the night for people who don't run on a normal calendar: students and Erasmus, travellers, expats, and everyone who works in hospitality and gets their weekend on Monday and Tuesday.

A Monday night in Barcelona, hour by hour

Barcelona runs late. A good Monday looks like this:

  • 21:30Late dinner. Poble-sec, right next to Sala Apolo, is full of places to eat, especially around Carrer de Blai.
  • 00:00Doors open at Sala Apolo. Bad Decisions starts.
  • 01:30Last call for the free list. After this you need a ticket.
  • 05:00Lights on. The metro opens again at 5am on weekdays, so getting home is easy.

Where is it and how to get there

Sala Apolo, Carrer Nou de la Rambla 113, 08004 Barcelona, in Poble-sec. It's one of the city's classic venues, a few minutes' walk from the Gothic Quarter and the Raval.

  • Metro: Paral·lel (L2 and L3), two minutes away.
  • On foot: about 15 minutes from Plaça de Catalunya, down the Rambla and along Nou de la Rambla.
  • Going home: we close at 5am, when the metro starts running again on weekdays.

What's the music and the crowd?

Throwbacks all night: the hip-hop, house and pop classics everyone knows, played by our resident DJ. No guest list drama, no strict dress code, no pretending.

The crowd is international and local, mostly 18 to 35: students, Erasmus, expats, travellers and hospitality workers. A good place to meet people if you've just arrived in Barcelona.

How to get in (for free)

Join the free list and get in free until 01:30. After that, an advance ticket is 11 € and a ticket at the door is 13 €. Bring a valid ID: it's 18+. No football or sports team shirts.
