How does the free list work?

Join the free list on DICE and get in before 01:30 without paying. After 01:30 you need a ticket: 11 € in advance or 13 € at the door.

When and where is it?

Every Monday night, from midnight to 5am, at Sala Apolo (C/ Nou de la Rambla 113, 08004 Barcelona). The opening is on Monday 1 March 2027.

What music do you play?

Throwbacks: hip-hop, house and pop classics, played by our resident DJ.

Is there a dress code?

Come as you are. Just no football or sports team shirts. The venue reserves the right of admission.

Do I need ID?

Yes. It's 18+ and you'll need a valid ID at the door.
